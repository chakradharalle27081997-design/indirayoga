Privacy policy

NaviraYoga Studio is based in Hyderabad, India, and currently offers live online yoga sessions. This notice describes the public website, not a complete policy for the separate student portal.

Information and purposes

When you contact us, we use the information you choose to provide to respond to enquiries and arrange sessions. Registration and student records are handled through the separate portal; its data collection and retention practices must be disclosed there before submission. Please do not send medical records through general enquiries.

Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures page visits and selected website interactions. Google may process device, browser, approximate location and usage information. Hosting providers may process technical request logs. We do not intentionally send registration form contents or health details as analytics event parameters. Article URLs may reveal topics of interest.

External services and choices

Links to WhatsApp and the student portal are subject to their respective privacy practices. You can decline or change analytics preferences at any time. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable obligations.
