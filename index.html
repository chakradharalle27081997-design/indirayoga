The Navira Method

A practice that evolves with you — not a routine you adapt to.

Most studios teach a fixed sequence. We start from your body: your conditions, your injuries, your goals — and design a plan around them. Every cycle, your instructor reviews your progress and adjusts your practice, so the yoga keeps meeting you where you actually are.
